Canada Food System Stress Test

Canada grows plenty of food. A few weak links could still turn one bad event into a crisis.

Banks are regularly tested against a severe but plausible bad year, so that weak points are fixed before a crisis. This prototype shows how Canada could do the same for its food system. It walks through four steps: how the system works, where it is exposed, what could hit it, and when acting early would still help.
