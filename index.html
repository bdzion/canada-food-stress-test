Canada Food System Stress Test

A proposed national platform to test threats before they strike and help decision-makers judge when to act.

Canada monitors many risks. What no one tests is how they combine across the whole food system, and what a late response costs. This prototype shows how a stress test could bring every major stress, at home and abroad, into one decision framework that supports the National Food Security Strategy and emergency management in agriculture: the warning signs, and the cost of acting early, reactively or not at all.
